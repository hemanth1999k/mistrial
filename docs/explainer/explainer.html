02 · why it slips through

Why nothing catches it today

01Eval platforms report one number and stop. They run the judge once per case. None report self-agreement, kappa between judges, or which cases flipped.
02The statistics exist, but not as a tool. Test-retest reliability, Cohen's kappa, Krippendorff's alpha: a century old, standard wherever humans rate things, stuck in notebooks.
03The projects that tried stop at the card. None take your existing evaluator, run in CI, or do anything about a flaky judge.
Before "how good is my model?" you need "can I trust the number that told me?"
